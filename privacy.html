DINK! 3D Privacy Policy

DINK! 3D does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, and are deleted when you delete the app. Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared with third parties, sold, or retained anywhere else. If a future version ever changes this, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
