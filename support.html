DINK! 3D Support

DINK! 3D is a first-person pickleball game that runs entirely on your device.

Getting started: the in-game tutorial covers movement, swinging, and serving. Career is a six-match tour, and Open Play sets up a single match.

Progress: your career, records, and settings save automatically on the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sound credits: the game uses Creative Commons sound recordings, credited in full inside the app under Settings.
